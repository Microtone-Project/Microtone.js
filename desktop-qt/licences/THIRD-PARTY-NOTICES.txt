------------------

Microtone's window, files, network, audio device handling and local server are
written against Qt, © The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors, and the Qt
libraries come with it as The Qt Company builds them, unmodified.

Licence: the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3
(licences/LGPL-3.0.txt), which builds on the GNU GPL version 3 (LICENSE.txt).
One module is not offered under the LGPL: Qt HTTP Server, which Qt offers under
the GNU GPL version 3 only (or commercially). Microtone's own licence is the

Microtone uses Qt's libraries as they are: Qt Core, Gui, Widgets, Network and

{{/windows}}

{{#macos}}

2. Qt WebEngine and Chromium
----------------------------

Microtone's window shows its pages through Qt WebEngine, which is Google's
Chromium inside Qt's interface to it. Qt WebEngine's own code is under the
licences of Qt (above); the Chromium part is under the Chromium Authors'
BSD-style licence and the licences of the components it is made of — ANGLE,
WebRTC, FFmpeg, ICU, HarfBuzz, BoringSSL, Opus, libvpx, dav1d, SQLite and over
a hundred more.

licences/qtwebengine-third-party.txt gives every one of them as Qt WebEngine
@QT_SERIES@ lists them: the component, its homepage, its licence and the
copyright lines. Chromium's own licence is the entry "Chromium License".

The licence that asks the most of a redistributor is the GNU LGPL version 2.1
(FFmpeg, in Chromium's media code: licences/LGPL-2.1.txt). Any GPL named in
that file is used only to reach Linux system resources, and Qt WebEngine
neither links to nor distributes GPL binary code. Like the rest of Qt, Qt
WebEngine is a library file you may replace (libQt6WebEngineCore, with its
helper QtWebEngineProcess and its resources) with your own build of the same
version, from the source:
https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/@QT_SERIES@/@QT_VERSION@/submodules/ — the archive
qtwebengine-everywhere-src-@QT_VERSION@.tar.xz holds Chromium's source as Qt
WebEngine builds it.


3. What Qt carries inside itself
--------------------------------

Qt's libraries hold a number of small libraries of other people's in their own
code (an image library, a regular-expression engine, a text shaper, hash
functions and the like). licences/qt-components.txt lists the ones in the Qt
modules above, with their versions, licences and copyright lines, from the
list of materials (SBOM) that Qt publishes with every module. It lists
the modules' components whether or not a given feature is used here, so it
may name more than the app loads. Each component's licence text is in Qt's
source, in the folder src/3rdparty/<name> of its module's archive (above).


4. RtAudio @RTAUDIO_VERSION@
----------------------------

Microtone plays through RtAudio, a cross-platform audio API,
© 2001–2023 Gary P. Scavone, compiled into the app.

Licence: the RtAudio licence, an MIT-style licence (licences/RtAudio-LICENSE.txt).
Source: https://github.com/thestk/rtaudio, tag @RTAUDIO_VERSION@. The copy in
Microtone's source (desktop-qt/vendor/rtaudio) carries two small patches to
the ASIO backend, marked MICROTONE PATCH and recorded in
desktop-qt/vendor/VENDOR-VERSIONS.md: a filter that keeps an ASIO driver that
crashes out of the app, and a forwarding for a driver that calls ASIO's
time-info callback.
{{#windows}}


5. Steinberg's ASIO SDK
-----------------------

The ASIO driver interface is Steinberg's: ASIO SDK 2.3, © Steinberg Media
Technologies GmbH, with the small changes RtAudio's release makes to a few of
its files (asiolist.cpp, asiodrivers.*). ASIO is a trademark and software of
Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH.

Licence: since 15 October 2025 Steinberg offers the ASIO SDK under the GNU
General Public License, version 3 (beside its proprietary licence), and
Microtone takes it under the GPL. That is why this Windows program, which
contains the SDK's code, is distributed under the GPL version 3
(LICENSE.txt, in full) and why its source is open: the SDK's files are in
desktop-qt/vendor/rtaudio/include of Microtone's source (address above),
unchanged from the RtAudio release.

An organisation that wants to distribute a version of Microtone under other
terms needs its own licence from Steinberg for ASIO.
