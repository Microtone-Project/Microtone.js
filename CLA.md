## In plain words

*This summary is here to help you read the agreement; it is not part of it.
Where the two differ, the numbered sections below are what counts.*

Microtone is free software: the tracker and its apps under the GNU General
Public License, version 3 or later, and the players (taudplay, libtaudplay,
Taudplay for Godot) under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or
later. Some people want to build Microtone's code into a product whose terms
the GPL or the LGPL do not allow — a closed-source game, a console title, a
hardware instrument — and for them the maintainer also offers the same code
under a separately agreed **proprietary licence**. That is called *dual
licensing*.

Dual licensing is only possible if the maintainer may license every part of the
code that way, including the parts you wrote. So before your contribution is
merged, we ask you to agree to this:

- **You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not a transfer. You may do
  whatever you like with your own work, elsewhere and under any terms.
- **Everyone gets your contribution under the project's free licence**, and the
  maintainer — the maintainer alone — may also offer it under a proprietary
  licence. Nobody who receives it, under either licence, may relicense it.
- **Beside, never instead.** Whatever the maintainer licenses on proprietary
  terms is code anyone can already get, complete, under the free licence. Your
  contribution is only ever licensed as part of the project, never on its own;
  there is no proprietary-only edition, no head start for paying licensees,
  and no exclusive deal.
- **Translations, songs and the fonts are left out.** A translation into another
  language, a song or demo project, and a change to the Motif typefaces are
  never put under a proprietary licence, so contributing only those does not
  need this agreement at all.
- **Small changes count too.** Whether a one-line fix is protected by copyright
  differs from country to country, so every contribution is covered the same
  way, however small.
- **Patents:** you license any patent of yours that your contribution needs. If
  someone sues claiming the project infringes a patent, they lose that patent
  licence.
- **You promise the work is yours to give**, and you tell us if part of it is
  not.
- **Korean law governs the agreement**, as the maintainer lives in Korea.
