register ramp — octave/period 0 … 9, computed in OKLCh from three stops

A spectral continuum: blue at the bottom of the keyboard, through cyan and green, to yellow at the top, with lightness running with it as a second, redundant channel. The eight registers between the stops are spaced by PERCEPTUAL distance, not by interpolation parameter — the ΔE row under each strip is the OKLab distance between neighbours, and the point is that it is flat. Tune --cv-oct-low / --cv-oct-mid / --cv-oct-high in css/microtone.css; an unreachable chroma is pulled back along constant lightness and hue rather than clipped per channel.

the plot itself — one lane, 32 rows, per theme

Top to bottom: a rising then falling scale; a repeated pitch; a PORTAMENTO over rows whose note column is blank (the contour must curve through it, not cut straight across); three ARPEGGIO rows, each drawing its other two pitches on a bar; a rest longer than RE_ENTRY_ROWS (the contour must BREAK); a leap of two periods (the band must re-root — the contour breaks and the tab changes colour). Glyphs are painted on top, as the grids paint them, so this is the legibility test: the plot has to stay behind the notation while its rose ink still reads as something other than notation.
